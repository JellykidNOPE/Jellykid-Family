# Jellykid character reference — October 24, 2026

The new canonical reference is `assets/jellykid-mascot.png` and the supplied portrait
in the sprite library's `brand-reference/` folder.

Jellykid has broad, sturdy shoulders and arms, a softly rounded midsection, and a less
round face. The intended build is “kinda fit, kinda fat.” Keep his navy cap, headphones,
brown beard, friendly expression, and blue JELLY KID shirt. Avoid an all-over chubby
shape, a thin athletic build, exaggerated muscles, or visible abdominal definition.

The October 24 imagegen edits preserve each artwork's existing pose, expression,
clothes, props, framing and text while adapting his proportions to that reference.
Distinct reaction poses and explorer gear remain available. Other characters and
creatures retain their established art. Earlier decks remain inactive, and the
temporarily hidden games remain hidden on the homepage.

This rollout covers websites, game art, the sprite library and Runway assets.
It does not change social-account profile pictures or previously published posts.
