# Jellykid social sprite pack

Compiled October 22, 2026 from the local active game collection.
Official website: https://jellykidgames.com/
Source repository: https://github.com/JellykidNOPE/grid-game
Source HEAD: 33e61f9e6e520599340ed54d39324471e5abf36c (compiled from the available artwork on October 22, 2026).

## Start here

Open this folder in a browser for the searchable visual library. You can download individual artwork files or the current-art ZIP.
Use the Download original button, or drag the files from the folders into your social editor.
Contact sheets are labeled previews; they are not the transparent artwork files.

256 catalog entries: 98 current artwork, 103 preserved alternates,
15 source/runtime copies, and 40 related illustrations/promotional art.
212 entries have actual alpha transparency; the rest retain their original background.
11 byte-identical filename aliases are listed separately rather than counted as additional poses.

## Folders

- 01-current-sprites: current Jellykid poses, Daily Six creatures, Dice & Dash units,
  Dungeon Step players and Dungeon Volley monsters.
- 02-cards-and-scenes: current High Card Havoc cards, Daily Six mimics and character scenes.
- 03-original-sources: source atlases, source artwork and native Dice & Dash atlas cells.
- 04-alternates: earlier Jellykid/AI decks, older Dice & Dash units and attack GIFs,
  saved-board Crawling Skull reactions, and preserved Late to Work human characters.
- 05-related-art: Late to Work people/animals/player cars and existing promotional scenes.
- contact-sheets: printable JPEG visual indexes. previews: gallery thumbnails only.

## Image notes

- Original file copies are unchanged, including alpha channels and GIF animation.
- Dice & Dash current sprites are the exact runtime files at 384 × 384. Higher-resolution
  source exports are exact 627 × 627 atlas quadrants. Source art crosses some cell boundaries,
  so raw atlas cells may include edges of adjacent characters. The original boss source is
  1230 × 1278. Both complete source atlases are preserved for manual editing.
- Every SVG is retained alongside a mechanical 2048px PNG render for editors that need PNG.
  PNG rendering adds resolution for scalable vectors, not detail to raster art.
- Gallery thumbnails trim empty transparent margins only. Original asset canvases are untouched.
- High Card Havoc has finished framed cards, not clean character cutouts. Daily Six mimic
  images include a dark background and partial numbering. Their backgrounds are preserved.
- Dungeon Volley only has 320 × 320 monster PNGs locally. No larger source masters were found.
- Current Dice & Dash animation is procedural in game. The supplied attack GIFs are earlier assets.
- Summit Quest and Don't Be Late to Work remain temporarily hidden from the homepage.
  Their available artwork is included here; the game files and website have not been modified.
- This is a collection of existing art, with no AI recreation, background removal or invented variants.
